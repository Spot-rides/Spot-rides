FAQ

Questions students ask us

Is it free?

Joining is free. You only share the fuel cost directly with the student you ride with.

Who can join?

Students of our pilot colleges in Hyderabad. We add more colleges as routes fill up.

How do you verify students?

A student volunteer checks your college ID on WhatsApp before adding you to a route group.

How much do I pay per ride?

₹3 per km, paid directly to the rider over UPI. A 10 km ride costs ₹30.

Is this a taxi service?

No. Students share rides they are already taking and split the fuel cost. Nobody earns a profit.

Is it safe for women?

You can choose women only rides, and every member is a verified student.

What if my ride cancels?

The rider posts a cancel update in the group so you can pick another ride on your route.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The pilot runs entirely on WhatsApp.

What happens to my data?

Your number is shared only with the riders you match with. Read the privacy policy at the bottom of this page.

Privacy policy

We collect your name, WhatsApp number, college, area and travel timings only to match you with students on your route. Your number is shared only with riders you match with. We never sell your data. To see or delete your data, email us at either address above.

Terms

Spot Rides connects verified students who share rides they are already taking. Riders share fuel costs at ₹3 per km and nobody earns a profit. Riders must hold a valid licence and insurance and carry a helmet for any pillion. Members follow the group rules and travel at their own discretion. Anyone who breaks the rules is removed.
